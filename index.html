How what we eat, and when, shapes health across generations

I am a nutritional and perinatal epidemiologist at National Taiwan University. My group studies maternal diet, chrono-nutrition and gestational diabetes, using pregnancy cohorts and continuous glucose monitoring across Asia and Europe.

What we study

More about our research

  • Chrono-nutrition and meal timing

    Whether the timing of meals matters alongside what is eaten: night eating, irregular meal times, fasting duration and time-restricted eating.

  • Gestational diabetes and continuous glucose monitoring

    Using minute-by-minute glucose data to describe glycemia across pregnancy and to predict gestational diabetes earlier.

  • Maternal diet and child health

    How diet in pregnancy relates to birth outcomes, body composition and cardiometabolic health in childhood.

  • Evidence synthesis

    Network, dose-response and individual participant data meta-analyses that answer questions single studies cannot.
