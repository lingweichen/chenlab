Local students. The College of Public Health at NTU admits PhD and Master’s students each year. If you are interested in nutritional or perinatal epidemiology, chrono-nutrition or continuous glucose monitoring, email me with your CV and a few sentences on what you would like to study.

International students. If you do not read or speak Chinese, you may wish to apply to the PhD or Master’s program offered by NTU’s Global Health Program instead. I would be glad to discuss supervision once you have been admitted.
